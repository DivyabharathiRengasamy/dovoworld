COMMON PROBLEMS

What usually holds Shopify stores back.

Duplicate & filter URLs

Collections, tags and filters create many URLs for the same products

Product pages reachable under several paths and filtered collection URLs can split ranking signals. We check canonicals, internal links and what Google actually indexes, then fix the template.

Theme & app bloat

Scripts that slow every page

Each app can add JavaScript that slows the store. We measure Core Web Vitals, find the heaviest scripts and recommend what to keep, defer or remove.

Structured data

Product markup that is missing or doubled

Themes and apps sometimes output overlapping Product schema. We test it and leave one clean, accurate version so price and availability can be read correctly.

HOW WE WORK

Fixes in order of impact, checked by a person.

Our AI systems handle the repetitive checking. A strategist reviews every recommendation before it reaches you. We do not guarantee rankings; we show you the evidence and report what moved.

  • 1. Crawl and index check

    Compare what is on your site with what Google has indexed, and list the gaps.

  • 2. Template fixes

    Collection, product and blog templates: titles, headings, canonicals and schema.

  • 3. Content and internal links

    Better collection copy and links that help shoppers and crawlers reach the right pages.

  • 4. Tracking

    GA4 ecommerce events and Search Console, so organic revenue is visible.

QUESTIONS

Shopify SEO, answered.

Do you work with Shopify Plus?

Yes. The approach is the same; Plus stores often have more markets, apps and custom checkout work to account for.

Will you change my theme?

Only where a change is needed for search or speed, and we agree it with you first. We aim for the smallest fix that solves the problem.

Can you help with selling in more than one country?

Yes. We review how your store handles regions, currencies and language versions, and what that means for search. See our market pages for UAE, US, UK and Australia.
