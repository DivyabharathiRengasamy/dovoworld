Reading creative fatigue before your CPM tells you

19 APR 2026 · 4 MIN READ · PERFORMANCE MEDIA

By the time cost per thousand impressions climbs, creative fatigue has usually been at work for a while. A few earlier signals give you time to refresh before results slip.

Signals that move first

Watch for these patterns across a creative's life, ideally compared against its own early performance:

  • Rising frequency among your core audience without matching growth in reach.
  • Click-through rate drifting down while impressions stay steady.
  • Falling engagement quality, such as shorter view time or fewer saves and shares.
  • Negative feedback or hide rates creeping upward.

Separate fatigue from other causes

Not every dip is fatigue. Seasonality, a landing page issue, tracking changes or increased competition can look similar. Check whether performance dropped across all creatives or only the oldest ones before deciding what to replace.

Build a refresh rhythm

Rather than waiting for a crisis, plan a steady flow of new variations. Change the hook or opening seconds first, then format and message, keeping what works recognisable. A small, regular pipeline of fresh concepts is easier to manage than an emergency overhaul.

Keep a simple log

Record each creative's launch date, key metrics at week one and week three, and when it was retired. Over a few months, that history shows how long your audience tolerates a concept and helps you time the next refresh.
