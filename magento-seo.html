COMMON PROBLEMS

What usually holds Magento stores back.

Layered navigation

Parameter URLs that multiply

Filters can create huge numbers of near-duplicate URLs. We set rules for what gets crawled and indexed and check them against real data.

Canonicals & sitemaps

Signals that disagree

Canonical tags, sitemaps and internal links should point the same way. We audit them and fix the conflicts.

Performance

Heavy front ends and caching gaps

Magento speed depends on theme, caching and hosting. We measure Core Web Vitals and give you a prioritised fix list your developers can act on.

HOW WE WORK

Fixes in order of impact, checked by a person.

Our AI systems handle the repetitive checking. A strategist reviews every recommendation before it reaches you. We do not guarantee rankings; we show you the evidence and report what moved.

  • 1. Crawl analysis

    Large-scale crawl compared with indexation and log or Search Console data.

  • 2. Rules

    Canonical, robots and sitemap rules for filters, search and parameters.

  • 3. Developer-ready fixes

    Clear tickets your development team can implement.

  • 4. Monitoring

    Ongoing checks so new releases do not undo the work.
