What Shopify SEO Services Include (and What Shopify Already Handles)

18 NOV 2026 · 10 MIN READ · SEO

Written by Divyabharathi, AI Automation Engineer & SEO Specialist

Quick answer: Shopify SEO services improve how a Shopify store is crawled, indexed, ranked and chosen in search and AI shopping results. They usually cover an index audit, collection and product template fixes, duplicate URL and app clean-up, Product schema, speed, international Markets setup, collection content, internal links and revenue tracking.

Shopify takes care of a lot of SEO groundwork for you. Every store gets an SSL certificate, an automatic sitemap, a default robots.txt file and canonical tags out of the box. That makes it easy to launch, and it also makes it easy to assume search is already handled.

It rarely is. The platform's fixed rules, your theme and the apps you install all shape how Google sees the store. This guide explains what professional Shopify SEO services cover, why each part matters, and what Shopify already does for you so you do not pay for work you do not need. To see the issues these services fix in practice, read our companion post on common Shopify SEO problems and how to fix them.

What Shopify Already Handles for You

According to Shopify's SEO overview, the platform automatically adds canonical tags to reduce duplicate content, generates sitemap.xml and robots.txt, includes the store name in title tags, enables SSL by default, and ships themes with Product schema markup.

These defaults are a good starting point. A Shopify SEO service checks that they are actually working on your theme, then focuses on the gaps the platform leaves open: content, structure, apps, speed and measurement.

What Shopify SEO Services Include

A thorough Shopify SEO service covers nine areas. The order below reflects where most stores see results first.

1. Index and Coverage Audit

The first step is to compare the pages in your store with the pages Google has actually indexed. Search Console's Page indexing report shows which products and collections are excluded and why. The audit also crawls the store for broken links, redirect gaps after deleted products, thin pages and duplicate titles, then ranks every issue by its likely effect on revenue.

2. Collection and Product Template Fixes

Shopify stores share templates, so one fix can improve hundreds of pages at once. The service reviews the collection, product and blog templates for heading structure, title and meta description patterns, canonical tags, breadcrumbs and image alt text. Fixing the template is faster and more reliable than editing pages one by one.

3. Duplicate URL and Internal Link Clean-Up

Shopify uses fixed URL paths such as /products/, /collections/ and /pages/. Many themes also link to products through their collection, creating addresses like /collections/shoes/products/trail-runner. Shopify's canonical tag points these back to the main /products/ URL, but internal links to the collection version still waste crawl effort. A good service updates the theme so links go straight to the canonical product address.

4. App and Script Audit

Apps add features, and many of them add JavaScript to every page. Over time, removed apps can leave code behind in the theme. The service lists every installed app, measures its effect on page speed, removes leftover snippets and replaces heavy apps with lighter options or native Shopify features where possible.

5. Product Schema and Rich Results

Shopify themes include Product schema, but SEO and review apps often add their own. The result can be two or three overlapping sets of markup with conflicting prices or ratings. The service keeps one accurate version with price, availability, brand, reviews and identifiers such as GTIN where you have them, then checks it in Google's Rich Results Test.

6. Speed and Core Web Vitals

Shopify hosts every store on its own infrastructure, so speed problems usually come from the theme, apps, oversized images, custom fonts or third-party scripts rather than the server. We test Core Web Vitals on the home, collection and product templates, then tackle the heaviest causes before anything else.

7. Collection Page Content

Collections are often the pages that can rank for broad, high-volume searches. Adding a clear introduction, helpful buying advice, well-chosen filters and links to related collections turns a product grid into a page that search engines and shoppers both understand.

8. International SEO with Shopify Markets

For stores selling in more than one country, Shopify Markets generates hreflang tags automatically for markets with their own domain, subdomain or subfolder, and serves translated pages at their own URLs. The service checks that each market is set up correctly, that currencies and languages match the right regions, and that translated content is complete rather than half in English.

9. AI Shopping Visibility and Tracking

Shopify's agentic storefronts make eligible products available to AI channels such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot and Google AI Mode, using your product titles, descriptions, options, images, price and availability. Complete, accurate product data therefore helps both classic search and AI shopping. Last, GA4 ecommerce tracking and Search Console are linked, so every sale from organic search is counted.

Frequently Asked Questions About Shopify SEO Services

What do Shopify SEO services include?

Shopify SEO services usually include an index audit, template fixes, duplicate URL and app clean-up, Product schema, speed improvements, collection content, international Markets setup and revenue tracking.

Is Shopify good for SEO?

Yes. Shopify handles SSL, sitemaps, canonical tags and basic schema automatically. Stores still need good content, clean templates and careful app choices to rank well.

Can I change URLs in Shopify?

You can edit the handle at the end of each URL, but Shopify's path prefixes such as /products/ and /collections/ are fixed. When you change a handle, add a redirect from the old address.

Does Shopify add product schema automatically?

Shopify themes include Product schema by default. Apps can add duplicate markup, so it is worth checking that only one accurate version appears on each product page.

Can I edit robots.txt in Shopify?

Yes, through a robots.txt.liquid template in your theme. Shopify warns that incorrect changes can remove a store from search, so edits should be made carefully and tested.

Do Shopify apps slow down my store?

Many apps add scripts to every page, and removed apps can leave code behind. Auditing apps is one of the most effective ways to speed up a Shopify store.

How long do Shopify SEO services take to show results?

Fixes to templates and indexing often show up in Search Console within a few weeks. Steady growth in rankings and sales typically takes three to six months.

Do Shopify SEO services work for Shopify Plus?

Yes. The approach is the same, with extra attention to multiple markets, more apps and custom checkout features.
