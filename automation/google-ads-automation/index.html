Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will the automation change my bids or budgets on its own?

No. It checks your account, raises alerts and prepares changes such as negative keywords or budget moves. Nothing is changed in Google Ads until you approve it, unless you agree a specific rule with us in writing.

What access do you need to my Google Ads account?

Read access is enough for alerts and reports. To apply approved changes, such as adding negative keywords or new ads, we need standard access. You stay the account owner.

Which campaign types does it cover?

Alerts and reports cover Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Display and YouTube campaigns. Search term clean-up and ad copy drafts are mainly for Search and Shopping campaigns.

How often does it check the account?

Every morning by default. You can choose a different schedule, and urgent problems, such as a campaign that suddenly spends far above its daily budget, are flagged straight away.

Does it write ads in Google's character limits?

Yes. Headlines and descriptions are drafted from your landing page within Google's limits for responsive search ads, and a person checks them for accuracy and policy issues before you approve them.
