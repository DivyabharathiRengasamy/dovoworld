What it measures

Counts, lengths, areas and checks.

Everything is worked out from the vector linework in your PDF and the scale on the drawing.

01 Counts

Every symbol on the drawing, counted and named.

The tool finds each repeated symbol, groups identical ones and names them from the legend.

  • Manholes, road gullies, inspection chambers and bollards
  • Light fittings, sockets, fans and other electrical symbols
  • Each item highlighted on the drawing so you can check it

04 Checks

Catches mistakes before they reach the price.

The tool compares what is drawn with what the legend and labels say, and flags anything that does not add up.

  • Legend quantity does not match the drawing
  • Symbols missing a reference label
  • Legend items that never appear on the drawing
  • No scale found on the sheet

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which drawings does the takeoff tool read?

PDF drawings exported from CAD software such as AutoCAD, Civil 3D, MicroStation or Revit. These keep the real linework, which the tool measures. Scanned or photographed drawings are not supported yet.

How does it know the scale?

It reads the scale from the title block, for example 1:200 or 1:100. If the scale is missing or wrong, you can type the correct scale and every length and area updates.

How accurate are the quantities?

On our sample drawings every count, length and area matched the known quantities. Real drawings vary in how they are drawn, so always review the results before you price from them.

Is my drawing uploaded to your servers?

No. The takeoff tool opens and measures the PDF inside your own browser. The drawing is not sent to DoVoWorld.

How does it name each item?

It matches symbols, line types and fills on the drawing with the ones in the drawing legend, and uses the legend text as the item name. Items that are not in the legend are listed as unnamed.

Can I export the takeoff?

Yes. Download the results as a CSV file that opens in Excel, with each item, its quantity and unit, the legend quantity and any issues found.
