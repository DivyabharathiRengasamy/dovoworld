Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which ecommerce platforms do you work with?

We work with stores on Shopify, WooCommerce and Magento, and with custom stores that can import product data. Tell us your platform and we will confirm the setup before we start.

Will you overwrite my product descriptions?

Only the ones you approve. Every new title, description and FAQ is shown next to the current version, and nothing changes in your store until you approve it.

How do you avoid duplicate content across similar products?

Copy is written from each product's own attributes, such as material, size, use and compatibility, so variants and similar products get different text instead of the manufacturer's description.

What about out-of-stock and discontinued products?

We flag them and suggest the right action for each one, for example keeping the page with alternatives, or redirecting it to the closest category.

How many products can you handle?

From a few hundred to tens of thousands. Large catalogues are done in batches, starting with the categories and products that bring in the most revenue.
