Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does DoVoWorld automate?

Two things. For marketing teams we automate SEO, ecommerce SEO, Google Ads and social media work on your live accounts. For civil engineering teams we take quantities off CAD PDF drawings: counts, lengths and areas.

Does anything change without my approval?

No. Marketing automations prepare the change and wait for you to approve it. The quantity takeoff tool only reads your drawing and gives you the numbers to check.

Where should I start?

Marketing teams usually start with a free crawl of their site. Civil engineering teams can try the takeoff tool on a sample drawing or on one of their own CAD PDFs.
