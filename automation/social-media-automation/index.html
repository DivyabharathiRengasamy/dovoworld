Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which social platforms do you cover?

LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and X. Posts are written for each platform's format and audience instead of copying the same text everywhere.

Will posts go out without me seeing them?

No. Each week's calendar is sent for approval first. Only approved posts are scheduled.

Where do the post ideas come from?

From your own content: blog posts, product launches, case studies, events and offers. That keeps social posts consistent with your website and sends traffic back to it.

Do you reply to comments and messages?

We sort incoming comments and messages and flag questions, complaints and sales leads to the right person on your team. Replies are written or approved by your team.

Do you create images and videos?

We suggest the visual for each post and can prepare simple graphics from your brand templates. Photo and video shoots are planned separately with our creative team.
