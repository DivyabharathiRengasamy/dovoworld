Privacy Policy

What we collect on dovoworld.com, why, and the choices you have. Last updated 24 October 2026.

Who we are

DoVoWorld is a digital marketing agency based in Bengaluru, India. You can reach us at hello@dovoworld.com.

What we collect

Contact form. If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email, company, topic and message you type. We use it only to reply to you and to discuss work with you. It is delivered to our team by email.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and buttons (such as WhatsApp, email and phone links) are used. It sets cookies and records approximate location and device information. For visitors in the UK, EU and Switzerland, analytics stays off until you accept it in the cookie banner.

WhatsApp, email and phone links. If you click through to WhatsApp, your email app or your phone, that service handles the conversation under its own privacy terms.

Site assistant. The "Falcon" chat widget on this site gives preset answers in your browser. It does not send what you type to us or to a third party.

Where your data is handled

This site is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare. Analytics is provided by Google. Both may process data outside your country, including in the United States and India.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterwards for our business records. Analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics retention settings.

Your choices and rights

You can accept or decline analytics cookies in the banner, and change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser. Depending on where you live (for example the UK, EU, Australia or California) you may have the right to ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Email hello@dovoworld.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
