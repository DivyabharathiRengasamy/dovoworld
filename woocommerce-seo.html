WooCommerce SEO without the plugin pile-up.

COMMON PROBLEMS

What usually holds WooCommerce stores back.

Plugin overhead

Too many plugins, too slow

Every extra plugin can add scripts and database work. We measure the real impact and help you cut what is not earning its place.

Archives & variations

Thin and duplicate pages

Tag, attribute and filter archives and product variations can create many low-value pages. We decide which should be indexed and set that up cleanly.

Hosting & caching

Speed limited by the server

Slow hosting and missing caching hold back Core Web Vitals. We review your setup and tell you plainly whether the fix is configuration or a better host.

HOW WE WORK

Fixes in order of impact, checked by a person.

Our AI systems handle the repetitive checking. A strategist reviews every recommendation before it reaches you. We do not guarantee rankings; we show you the evidence and report what moved.
