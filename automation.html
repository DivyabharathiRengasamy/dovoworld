    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    What does the crawler actually change?

    It reads every page's title, meta description and H1, flags the ones that are missing, duplicated, too long or too short, and writes better versions. Only the versions you approve are published to your site.

    Does anything go live without my approval?

    No. Every title, blog post, landing page and internal link waits for your approval. Each published change is logged, so you can see what changed and when.

    Which websites can you publish to?

    We work with sites built on common platforms such as WordPress and Shopify, and with custom sites through their code repository. Tell us what your site runs on and we will confirm the setup.

    Will AI-written content hurt my rankings?

    Google judges content on whether it is helpful, not on how it was written. Every post and landing page is researched against real searches, reviewed by a person and edited before it goes live.

    How is it priced?

    It depends on the size of your site and how many posts and pages you want each month. Start with a free crawl and we will recommend a plan.
