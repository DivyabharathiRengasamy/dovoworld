QUESTIONS

Marketing automation FAQs.

What can you automate for a marketing team?

We set up five core workflows: lead capture and alerts, SEO audit reports, content brief generation, ad performance alerts and weekly client reports. We can adapt them to the tools you already use.

Which tools do you build on?

We build on established automation platforms such as Make or n8n, chosen per project, and connect them to your CRM, analytics, ad accounts and email. You keep ownership of your own accounts and data.

Does a person review the automations?

Yes. A strategist designs each workflow, tests it with your real data and checks the outputs, so the automation supports your team's judgement instead of replacing it.

What do you need from me to get started?

Read access to the accounts the workflow touches, such as analytics, ad platforms or your CRM, plus a short call to agree what a good result looks like for you.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We don't promise rankings, leads or revenue numbers. We show you the plan, the workflow and the data it runs on, and report honestly on what it does.

Tell us which task eats your week.

A short conversation and a straight read on what is worth automating first. No guaranteed numbers.
