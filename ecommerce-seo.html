WHAT WE FIX

Three problems that cap most online stores.

Speed & crawl

Slow pages and wasted crawl

Heavy templates, third-party scripts and thousands of near-duplicate URLs slow shoppers down and waste Google's attention. We audit Core Web Vitals, indexation and crawl paths, then rank fixes by likely impact.

Product & category pages

Pages that cannot earn traffic

Thin descriptions, weak titles, missing Product structured data and URLs that compete with each other. We rebuild templates and internal linking, starting with the checks in our cannibalisation audit checklist.

Revenue tracking

Rankings that nobody can tie to money

We set up GA4 ecommerce events and Search Console so you can see organic revenue, leads and the pages that drive them, instead of a rank report with no business meaning.

HOW IT WORKS

From audit to weekly reporting, with a person checking the work.

Our AI systems layer does the repetitive work: crawling, checking structured data, spotting duplicate pages and watching your search data. A strategist who knows ecommerce decides what is worth doing.

QUESTIONS

Straight answers before you reach out.

Do you guarantee rankings or revenue?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee a ranking. We give you a plan, show the data behind each recommendation and report what moved, so you can judge the work on evidence.

How long does ecommerce SEO take to work?

Technical fixes such as speed and indexation can show up in crawl and performance data within weeks. Ranking and revenue changes usually take longer and depend on your competition and starting point, so we set expectations after the audit rather than before it.

Which platforms do you work with?

Shopify, WooCommerce and Magento are our focus. If you run something else, send us the store address and we will tell you straight whether we are a good fit.

What do I get from the free audit?

A first-pass read of your store covering speed, indexation, structured data and the highest-impact fixes, reviewed by a strategist. There is no obligation to continue.

Can you also handle ads, social media and chat automation?

Yes. The same team runs performance media, content, social and marketing automation, including AI chat assistants, so search work and revenue work stay connected.
